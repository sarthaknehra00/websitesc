About Us

More than a school. A community built for every child.

Global Autism Learning School is a leading autism-focused institution in Delhi dedicated to empowering children on the autism spectrum through expert education and therapy.

Founded by educators and allied health professionals, our integrated model ensures that therapy and education happen seamlessly side-by-side — because we believe that when the environment adapts to the child, true potential is unlocked.
